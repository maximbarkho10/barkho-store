About

Hi, I'm Maxim. BARKHO is my own family name — an Assyrian surname carried from an ancient civilization into a small label I'm building today from Norway. The emblem on every piece draws from real Assyrian iconography: a golden sun disc, the same kind of mark found at the heart of the modern Assyrian flag, reworked here as a personal signature. Everything is made only after you order it — no stockpiles, no guessing what sells, just the pieces you actually chose.
