Our story

A family name, carried forward.

BARKHO is my family name. I'm Maxim, an Assyrian living in Norway, and I started this label so we can wear our heritage with pride, wherever we live today.

The rosette on every piece comes from ancient Assyrian palace art, carved into the walls of Nineveh and Nimrud. We embroider it in a thread colour chosen for each garment, and make each piece only after you order it.
