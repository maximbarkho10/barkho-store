Shipping

Every piece is printed or embroidered only after you order it. Once production is done, your order ships and you receive a shipping notice. We ship to Norway, Sweden, Denmark, Finland, Germany, the Netherlands, the United Kingdom and the United States for 99 NOK per order. Orders of 1,200 NOK or more (before discount codes) ship free to these countries.

We also ship to the United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Jordan, Qatar, Kuwait and Turkey for 179 NOK per order. Delivery to these countries usually takes 2–4 weeks in total.

Orders to countries outside the EU/EEA may be subject to local import duties or taxes charged by your country's customs. These are paid by the customer on delivery and are not included in our prices.

Returns — 14-day right of withdrawal

Changed your mind? You can return your order within 14 days of receiving it.

  • Email maxim.barkho@hotmail.com within 14 days, with your order details.
  • Send the items back within 14 days after that. You pay the return shipping.
  • Items should be unworn beyond trying on, unwashed and in their original condition.
  • We refund the item price and the original shipping cost within 14 days, to the card you paid with.

Damaged or wrong item

If your item arrives damaged, with a print or embroidery fault, or in the wrong size or product, email us a photo and your order details. We will send a replacement or refund you — you pay nothing. You can report a fault up to two years after receiving the item.
